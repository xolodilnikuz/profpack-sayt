Paper types

  • Testliner

    Paper for the flat layers of corrugated board, outer and inner. It forms the board surface, and printing goes on this layer.

  • Fluting

    Paper for the corrugated layer. It passes between fluted rolls, takes on a wave shape and gives the board its rigidity.

How the paper is made

The raw material is waste paper sorted by GOST 10700-97 grades and cleaned of film, metal, staples and other contaminants.

The waste paper is shredded and pulped with water in a hydropulper.

The pulp is cleaned, then pressed and dried on the paper machine and wound into rolls.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between testliner and fluting?

Testliner is paper for the flat layers of corrugated board; fluting is for the corrugated layer. Both are made from waste paper.

What roll formats (widths) are available?

Formats are made to order. Tell us the width and volume you need and we will confirm what is possible and when.

How much do paper rolls cost?

The price depends on volume and technical requirements. Send a request or call us for a quote.
