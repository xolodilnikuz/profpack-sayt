How corrugated board is built

Corrugated board is a fluted layer (fluting) bonded with starch adhesive between flat layers (testliner).

One fluted layer makes 3-layer board (T grades); two make 5-layer board (P grades).

The flutes make the board light yet resistant to bending and compression, which is why corrugated board is the main packaging material.

Layers, flutes and grades

  • 3-layer (T)

    One fluted layer between two flat liners. For packaging light and medium-weight goods.

  • 5-layer (P)

    Two fluted layers: a stronger board for heavy loads and high stacks.

  • E, B and C flutes

    The flute profile is the wave height. Per GOST 7376-89: E is 1.1–1.6 mm, B is 2.2–3.2 mm, C is 3.2–4.4 mm.

  • Choosing a grade

    Within one class, the higher the grade number, the higher the edge crush resistance. We choose the grade by load weight and stacking height.

Frequently asked questions

How much does corrugated board cost?

The price depends on the grade: a corrugated sheet (blank) costs from 2,300 to 7,000 UZS per m². Enter the sheet length and width in the calculator to see the price per piece and for the whole batch.

What is the difference between 3-layer and 5-layer board?

3-layer board (T grades) has one fluted layer between two flat liners; 5-layer board (P grades) has two. Within one class, the higher the grade number, the higher the edge crush resistance: per GOST 7376-89, for example, at least 3.8 kN/m for T-23 and at least 6.0 kN/m for P-32.

What is the difference between E, B and C flutes?

The flute profile is the height of the corrugated wave. Per GOST 7376-89: E is 1.1–1.6 mm, thin and smooth for printing, used for small and consumer packaging; B is 2.2–3.2 mm, the most widely used and crush-resistant; C is 3.2–4.4 mm and stronger when stacked. Double-wall BC and EB profiles are 5-layer board for heavy loads.

What is the minimum order and lead time?

They depend on the design, printing and run length. Send a request and we will tell you the exact minimum batch and lead time.
