What we print on boxes

  • Logo and design

    Your brand mark, name and house-style artwork, so your product stands out on the shelf and in the warehouse.

  • Markings

    The product name, weight, manufacturer details and other required text go straight onto the box.

  • Barcodes and QR codes

    Codes for warehouse tracking and retail are printed on the box, so there is no need for a separate label.

  • Handling symbols

    Symbols such as “This way up”, “Fragile” and “Keep dry”, so the load is carried and stored correctly.

What flexo printing is

Flexography (flexo printing) is the most common way to print on corrugated board: the image is transferred onto the board from a flexible printing plate with fast-drying inks.

It is fast and economical for long runs, and text and symbols come out crisp. Colours follow your artwork.

To order, send your logo or finished artwork and tell us the box size and run length. We agree the print layout and quote the price and lead time.

Frequently asked questions

Do you print logos on boxes?

Yes. Your logo, design and markings are flexo-printed on the boxes, including barcodes, QR codes and handling symbols. Send your artwork: the colours follow the artwork.

How much does printing on boxes cost?

The price depends on the box size, the print colours and the run length. Send your artwork and quantity and we will quote the price and lead time.

Can I order boxes in my own size?

Yes. Boxes are made to the customer’s dimensions: send the length × width × height, the product weight and the quantity, and we will choose the board grade and design (FEFCO).

What is the minimum order and lead time?

They depend on the design, printing and run length. Send a request and we will tell you the exact minimum batch and lead time.
