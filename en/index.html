01

Waste paper intake

Old cardboard boxes, printed board, books and magazines, office paper and newspapers arrive at the plant. Waste paper is sorted by GOST 10700-97 grades and cleaned of film, metal, staples and other contaminants.

  • MS-5B — corrugated board
  • MS-6B — printed board
  • MS-7B — books, magazines
  • MS-8V — newspapers
02

Recycling and paper rolls

Waste paper is shredded and pulped with water in a hydropulper. The pulp is cleaned, then pressed and dried on the paper machine and wound into rolls: testliner for the flat layers, fluting for the corrugated layer.

  • Shredding
  • Stock preparation
  • Pressing and drying
  • Winding into rolls
03

Corrugated board production

On the corrugator, fluting passes between fluted rolls, takes on a wave shape and is bonded to testliner layers with starch adhesive. One fluted layer makes 3-layer board, two make 5-layer board.

  • 3-layer (T)
  • 5-layer (P)
  • Starch adhesive
04

Box making

Corrugated sheets are cut to size, creased, die-cut where needed and glued. From the standard four-flap box (FEFCO 0201) to self-assembly boxes, trays and partitions.

  • FEFCO 0201
  • FEFCO 0427
  • Trays and partitions
05

Printing — your brand on the box

Your logo, design and markings are flexo-printed on the box, including handling symbols, barcodes and QR codes. A used box becomes waste paper again, and the loop is closed.

  • Flexo printing
  • Logo and markings
  • Barcodes and QR codes

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between E, B and C flutes?

The flute profile is the height of the corrugated wave. Per GOST 7376-89: E is 1.1–1.6 mm, thin and smooth for printing, used for small and consumer packaging; B is 2.2–3.2 mm, the most widely used and crush-resistant; C is 3.2–4.4 mm and stronger when stacked. Double-wall BC and EB profiles are 5-layer board for heavy loads.

What is the difference between 3-layer and 5-layer board?

3-layer board (T grades) has one fluted layer between two flat liners; 5-layer board (P grades) has two. Within one class, the higher the grade number, the higher the edge crush resistance: per GOST 7376-89, for example, at least 3.8 kN/m for T-23 and at least 6.0 kN/m for P-32.

How much weight can a box hold?

It depends on the board grade, box size and pallet stacking height. Box compression strength (BCT) is calculated from the board’s edge crush resistance (ECT). Tell us the product weight and storage conditions and we will choose the right grade and design.

What does FEFCO 0201 mean?

FEFCO is the international code of corrugated packaging designs. The first two digits are the group: 02 — slotted boxes, 04 — folder-type boxes and trays, 09 — inserts and partitions. 0201 is the most common four-flap box, and 0427 is a glue-free self-assembly box. Dimensions are always given as length × width × height.

What is the minimum order and lead time?

They depend on the design, printing and run length. Send a request and we will tell you the exact minimum batch and lead time.

How should I prepare waste paper for delivery?

Collect cardboard and paper separately and keep them dry: per GOST 10700-97, moisture must not exceed 15%. Remove film, tape, staples and food residue. Large volumes are easiest to tie into bales; we agree delivery terms by phone.
