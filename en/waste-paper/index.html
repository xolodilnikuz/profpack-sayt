What waste paper we accept

GOST 10700-97 requirements: moisture no more than 15%, one grade per bale, no wire, staples, sand or food residue.

We accept

  • Corrugated board and used boxes — MS-5B
  • Printed board and cardboard packaging — MS-6B
  • Books, magazines and notebooks (without covers) — MS-7B
  • Office paper and archive documents
  • Newspapers — MS-8V

We do not accept

  • Film- or polyethylene-coated paper, Tetra Pak
  • Waxed, greasy and bitumen-coated paper
  • Foil, metallised paper and photo paper
  • Till receipts (thermal paper)
  • Wet, burnt or rotten waste paper

What your waste paper becomes

Waste paper is used paper and cardboard: old boxes, cardboard packaging, books and magazines, office paper and newspapers.

At the plant it is sorted by grade, cleaned of film, metal and staples, then shredded and pulped.

The pulp becomes paper rolls, then corrugated board and new boxes, so the waste paper you hand in turns back into packaging. Profpack recycles about 2,500 tonnes of waste paper a month.

Frequently asked questions

How much do you pay for waste paper?

The price depends on the grade and volume. Tell us what waste paper you have and how much, and we will give you an exact price by phone: +998 95 946 00 00.

What waste paper do you accept?

Corrugated board and used boxes (MS-5B), printed board and cardboard packaging (MS-6B), books, magazines and notebooks (MS-7B), office paper, archive documents and newspapers (MS-8V). We do not accept film- or polyethylene-coated paper, waxed or greasy paper, foil, till receipts, or wet or burnt waste paper.

How should I prepare waste paper for delivery?

Collect cardboard and paper separately and keep them dry: per GOST 10700-97, moisture must not exceed 15%. Remove film, tape, staples and food residue. Large volumes are easiest to tie into bales; we agree delivery terms by phone.

Where do you accept waste paper?

At the plant: 3 Kanora St., Yangihayot District, Tashkent. Working hours: daily, 08:00–18:00. Please call before you come: +998 95 946 00 00.
